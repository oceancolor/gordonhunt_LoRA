## ---------- Suggested inference settings (SDXL) ----------
LoRA weight   : 0.65 - 0.90 (above 1.0 hardens texture and locks composition)
CFG           : 5 - 7
Sampler/steps : DPM++ 2M Karras / 28-35
Hires fix     : denoise ~0.45 (too high smears the dots into mush)
Glint density : prompt modifier OR weight adjustment, never both at once

## ---------- Stop criteria ----------
Pick the EARLIEST checkpoint that scores >= 80 and still responds to T2/T3.
If later checkpoints look more like the training images but lose flexibility, stop.
Overtraining signs: repeated backgrounds, locked compositions, one smeared palette
for every prompt, harsh texture, colour drift, T4 leakage.
